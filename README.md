\# Bike Sales Performance Analysis — Tableau \& Power BI



Sales Performance | Profitability | Product | Customer | Geographic Analysis



\## 📊 Project Overview



This project analyses bike sales transaction data from 2011–2016 to evaluate revenue performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, and geographic markets.



The project was developed using \*\*Excel, Tableau, Power BI, and DAX\*\*, with a focus on turning raw sales data into interactive dashboards and actionable business insights.



\## 🎯 Business Objective



The objective of this analysis is to identify:



\- Revenue and profit trends over time

\- The product categories and sub-categories driving sales

\- Products with strong revenue and profitability

\- Geographic markets with high revenue and strong margins

\- Key customer age groups and gender distribution

\- Opportunities to improve profitability and product mix



\## ❓ Business Questions



1\. How are revenue and profit performing over time?

2\. Which product categories generate the most revenue?

3\. Which products and sub-categories are most profitable?

4\. Which countries generate the highest revenue?

5\. Which markets have the strongest profit margins?

6\. Which age groups contribute most to revenue?

7\. How is revenue distributed by gender?

8\. Where are the main opportunities for margin improvement?

\## 🔍 Key Findings



\### Product Category Performance



\- \*\*Bikes generated approximately 72.5% of total revenue\*\*, making them the primary revenue driver.

\- \*\*Accessories generated approximately 58.6% profit margin\*\*, making them the strongest margin category.

\- Clothing contributed a smaller share of revenue and contained several relatively low-margin products.



\### Product \& Sub-Category Performance



\- \*\*Road Bikes\*\* generated the highest revenue among bike sub-categories.

\- \*\*Mountain Bikes\*\* achieved a stronger profit margin than Road Bikes despite generating lower revenue.

\- Helmets were among the strongest-margin product groups.



\### Geographic Performance



\- The \*\*United States\*\* generated the highest revenue.

\- \*\*Canada\*\* generated the highest overall profit margin among the countries analysed.

\- Geographic performance therefore differs depending on whether the focus is revenue scale or profitability.



\### Customer Demographics



\- Adults aged \*\*35–64\*\* contributed approximately 49.9% of total revenue.

\- Revenue was relatively balanced between male and female customers.

\- Demographic analysis is based on sales records containing the available age-group and gender fields; it does not represent unique customer counts.



\### Revenue Trend



Revenue increased substantially across the period analysed.



Because \*\*2016 only contains data through July\*\*, full-year 2016 should not be directly compared with full-year 2015.



For a like-for-like January–July comparison, 2016 revenue was approximately \*\*25.2% higher than January–July 2014\*\*, while profit was approximately \*\*20.0% higher\*\*.



\## 💡 Business Recommendations



Based on the analysis:



1\. \*\*Use Bikes as the primary volume driver\*\* while monitoring their profitability by sub-category and product.

2\. \*\*Increase accessory cross-selling and bundling opportunities\*\* because Accessories provide substantially higher margins.

3\. \*\*Investigate lower-margin clothing products\*\* to identify pricing, cost, or product-mix opportunities.

4\. \*\*Review Road Bike profitability\*\* to understand why its revenue leadership does not translate into the strongest margin.

5\. \*\*Analyse high-margin markets such as Canada\*\* to identify pricing, product-mix, or customer factors that could provide useful lessons for other markets.

6\. \*\*Monitor revenue and margin together\*\*, rather than evaluating growth based on revenue alone.
